## A Cybersecurity Incident I Recently Read About
In August 2026, McKesson, a major U.S. healthcare and pharmaceutical distribution company  
disclosed a cybersecurity incident after discovering unauthorized access to third-party 
applications connected to its systems. The extortion group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility, 
alleging that millions of patient records had been stolen. McKesson reported the breach in an 
SEC filing, as required for publicly traded companies facing material security incidents.

I found this interesting because it shows how breaches often don't come through a company's 
own core systems, but through third-party or vendor applications with weaker security — making 
supply-chain risk one of the biggest challenges in cybersecurity today. It also highlights why 
healthcare data is such a high-value target: patient records can't be "reset" like a password, 
so a leak has long-term consequences for victims.
